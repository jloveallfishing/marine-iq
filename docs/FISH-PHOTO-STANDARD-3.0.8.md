# MarineCast 3.0.8 photo review

## Primary identification resources

[RecFIN Fish ID](https://www.recfin.org/resources/fish-id/) provides individual downloadable JPEGs and asks users to credit their sources. Its credits identify several separate contributors, including Vicky Okimura/WDFW (Black, Blue, Cabezon, greenling and Lingcod), Ralph Pace (Cowcod, Gopher and Olive) and Milton Love (Bronzespotted). RecFIN’s download page does not explicitly grant commercial redistribution rights for these contributor photos. The page also carries a PSMFC rights-reserved notice. MarineCast links to the collection. Embedding or bundling those JPEGs awaits confirmation covering the individual assets.

[CDFW’s fish identification page](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Fish-ID/Common) links comparative charts, including the [Yellowtail and Olive diagnostic chart](https://nrm.dfg.ca.gov/FileHandler.ashx?DocumentID=213022&inline=). The published chart includes Widow. MarineCast keeps the chart as a reference and shows only Yellowtail and Olive in the comparison. The [CDFW Coho identification flyer](https://nrm.dfg.ca.gov/FileHandler.ashx?DocumentID=36125&inline=) independently documents California’s no-retention rule. Agency publication alone did not establish commercial extraction rights, so chart photos are linked rather than extracted.
