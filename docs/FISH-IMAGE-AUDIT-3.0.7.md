## Comparisons reviewed

Reviewed all six comparison groups against the [CDFW and RecFIN guide index](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Fish-ID) and [ODFW Black, Blue and Deacon guide](https://dfw.state.or.us/mrp/FishID/docs/Rockfish_ID_blk_blu_deacon.pdf). Black has a separate photo from Blue. The prior aquarium Deacon image was originally uploaded as Blue, and a reliable independent reidentification could not be confirmed. The Deacon card links to the ODFW guide and the original taxonomic paper through Identification guides. The scientific names and key characters are shown beside each comparison.

## Ocean salmon gaps

Neither ocean Chinook nor ocean Coho has a verified in-app image. The Cold Bay derby Coho candidate is excluded because USFWS identifies the derby as exclusive to Russell Creek. The NOAA observer manual and the USFWS-hosted chart have body, gum and tail photos, but reproduction rights for the individual relevant images were not established. The FDA RFE candidate was also excluded because its reuse notice does not clearly grant commercial reproduction and the marine phase was not confirmed. MarineCast links to the original guides and retains separate river photos.
