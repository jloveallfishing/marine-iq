# Personal Catch Tracker

Open Catch Tracker from Home or More. Set the trip location and Pacific date, choose the applicable waters and groundfish method, then add the species you want to count. Retained counts are prominent. The trip totals and any tracked combined groundfish cap stay visible while scrolling. Expand Released to record releases separately or remove a species. Removing recorded catches requires confirmation. Complete the trip and save it to the fishing log when ready. A completed trip can still be resumed before saving.

MarineCast stores one active or completed draft on the device. A completed trip becomes one fishing-log entry with species-level counts and a stable trip ID. Retrying the same save reuses that ID. Existing log entries and backups still work. Backups include completed trips saved in the log. The active draft stays in this browser and persists through app updates and offline reloads.

The tracker reuses the Rules snapshot and its scope, valid dates and verification window. A current numeric cap is not a determination that retention is legal. The tracker counts same-date retained fish from saved tracker trips, including other locations, against displayed caps. Untracked catches and manual log entries need to be considered separately. Released fish do not count against retained limits. Unidentified rockfish count toward the combined cap and suppress potentially misleading individual allowances.

The current snapshot covers only the species, regions and dates published in Rules. Published reference text, ambiguous numeric limits and expired rules cannot produce remaining counts. River salmon require reach-specific rules and have no computed allowances in this tracker. Groundfish combined counts require a method and a supported regulatory area. Fish photos come from the existing Rules catalog and may require a connection to load.

## 3.1.2 California groundfish review

CDFW's San Francisco Bay District page refers anglers to the San Francisco Management Area groundfish table. That table applies the statewide 10-fish rockfish, cabezon and greenling cap and lists lingcod separately. The tracker now includes Bay boat trips when the waters, method and dates are applicable. The existing verification window still applies. The combined total includes same-date retained catches stored in the tracker. Check local closures, depth and method restrictions before retaining fish. [CDFW Bay District](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Regulations/Fishing-Map/SF-Bay) · [CDFW groundfish table](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Regulations/Groundfish-Summary) · [CDFW in-season notices](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Regulations/Inseason) · [Federal groundfish rules](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/chapter-VI/part-660/subpart-G/section-660.360)

The Rockfish group organizes species by type. Cabezon and greenlings appear in Other supported species and still count toward the legal combined cap. Lingcod keeps its separate limit. The sticky combined warning stays visible when the Rockfish group is collapsed.
