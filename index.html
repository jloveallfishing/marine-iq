Map data & limitations

Arrows point toward movement, not wind origin. Colors indicate speed or tidal phase, not safety. Tide heights relative to MLLW. Tap a symbol for station and sample details. Wind particles and heatmap colors are interpolated from NWS grid forecasts where surrounding samples are available. Gust symbols show forecast samples. NOAA tide and current predictions apply at the named station and time. Current motion stays near that station. Not for navigation.

    GPS alerts and offline use

    Alerts are approximate and run only while MarineCast is visible. Proximity uses about 500 ft plus reported GPS uncertainty. Entry requires two fresh fixes inside after a fix outside. Poor or stale fixes pause warnings. A lack of warnings does not establish that an area is open or safe.

    Boundaries can be used offline after loading this map while online. Basemap tiles, online place search, and current regulations require a connection. Your GPS fixes are processed on this device.
